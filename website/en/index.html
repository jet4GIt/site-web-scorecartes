Card game scores, without the headache.

Score Cartes keeps score for SkyJo, Take 5! (Six qui prend), Flip 7, Papayoo, UNO, Belote or any card game. Photograph your cards: the app reads them right on your iPhone and works out the score.

📷

Card scanning

For SkyJo, Take 5!, Flip 7 and UNO, take a photo of your cards: the values are read on the device, with no connection. A doubtful card is flagged in orange; one tap to confirm or correct it. 10 free scans, then unlimited with Premium.

🃏

Six games, and yours

SkyJo, Take 5!, Flip 7, Papayoo, UNO and Belote have their rules built in. The Free game keeps score for any other game: Rummy, Tarot…

🏆

Ranking and end of game

The game ends by itself at the target or after a set number of rounds, and the winner is shown. Ties are settled by rounds won. In the Free game, players can also decide when it ends.

🎉

Game nights

Group the games of one night together, filter the list and the statistics on it, and see who dominated the night.

📊

Statistics

By player, game or game night: games and rounds won, average per round, best and worst round, best and worst game. With Premium.

📄

Shareable results

Share the results of a game or a game night as a PDF. The summary is free; with Premium, the full report adds statistics, round-by-round details and a short debrief of your games.

👥

Players and groups

Your usual players are remembered, you build groups, and the dealer rotates by itself from round to round.

📖

Rules at hand

The rules of each game are in the app, right when you need them: settle a disagreement in two taps.

☁️

iCloud, in three languages

Your games show up on your other devices through your iCloud. The app is in French, English and German.

Free and Premium

  • FreeEvery game, manual entry, game nights, iCloud, 10 free card scans and the results summary to share
  • PremiumA one-time purchase, no subscription: unlimited scans, statistics and the full report

Your data stays yours

Score Cartes has no account and no server. Your games are saved on your device and, if you wish, synced through your own iCloud, which the publisher cannot access. Card photos are analysed on the device and never kept, and there are no ads and no tracking.
