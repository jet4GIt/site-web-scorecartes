Score Cartes Support

Frequently asked questions and contact

Score Cartes keeps score for your card games, with or without card scanning. Here are the answers to the most common questions; for anything else, write to us directly.

Getting started

How do I create a game?

On the “Games” screen, tap “+”, choose the game, enter the players, set the target if needed, then tap “Create”. The player order is the playing order: the dealer rotates in that order, and you choose who deals the first round.

How do I enter the scores of a round?

In the game, tap “New round”: enter each player's score on the keyboard. In SkyJo, the “±” key above the keyboard adds or removes the minus sign. Then tap “Save”. When the target is reached, the game ends and the winner is shown.

Which games are supported?

SkyJo, Take 5! (Six qui prend), Flip 7, Papayoo, UNO and Belote have their rules built in (target, ranking order, doubling, belote, capot…). The “Free game” keeps score for any other game: you name it, choose whether the lowest or the highest score wins, and whether the game ends at a points target, after a set number of rounds, or when the players decide.

How does UNO work?

The first player with no cards left wins the round and scores the points of the cards left in the others' hands: each number is worth its value, Skip, Reverse and Draw Two are worth 20, Wild and Wild Draw Four are worth 50. Choose the winner, then give the total of the remaining cards: by scan, with the calculator, on the keyboard, or player by player. The game ends at 500 points, adjustable when you create it.

Card scanning

How does scanning work, and for which games?

Scanning is available in SkyJo, Take 5!, Flip 7 and UNO; 10 scans are free, after that you need Premium. In “New round”, tap the camera icon next to a player and photograph their cards: the values are read, the score is worked out, and you can keep entering the other players while the analysis runs. In UNO, a single photo is enough: the one of all the other players' remaining cards, because only the winner of the round scores. The “Calculate” button in the game also counts a score without saving it.

How do I photograph the cards well?

Good light, no glare on the cards; all cards face up, well separated and with no other card in the frame; the phone held flat above, with all the cards in the picture. In Take 5!, the numbers must be visible; in Flip 7, lay out the bonus and action cards too; in UNO, lay out all the remaining cards of the other players together, on a plain table.

A card is outlined in orange, or the scan got it wrong: what do I do?

An orange card is a doubtful reading: tap it to confirm or correct its value, and the score updates right away. You can also add a forgotten card or remove an extra one. In SkyJo, the grid has columns of 3 cards (12, 9, 6, 3): if the number of cards read is not a multiple of 3, the app tells you, since a card may have been missed or counted twice.

Does scanning need a connection?

No. Reading happens entirely on your iPhone, with no connection, and photos are never sent or kept. Details in the privacy policy.

Free and Premium

What is free, and what needs Premium?

Free: every game, manual entry, ranking, history, game nights, iCloud sync, 10 free card scans and a summary of the results to share. Premium, as a one-time purchase with no subscription: unlimited scans, statistics, the “Calculate” button during a game and the full report to share (statistics, round-by-round details, debrief).

I've used my 10 free scans: what can I do?

Manual entry is still available in every game: only photo scanning needs Premium. The number of scans left is shown in Settings → Premium.

How do I buy Premium, and get it on another device?

Tap “Go Premium” in Settings, or “Discover Premium” on a restricted feature. The purchase is one-time and carries over to your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. If needed, tap “Restore purchases” on the Premium screen, after a reinstall for example.

The purchase isn't working, or I want a refund

Check that you are signed in to the App Store and that purchases are not restricted in the iPhone's Settings (Screen Time). Payments and refunds are handled by Apple: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you run into trouble, write to us at the contact address below.

Understanding the game screen

What do the symbols in the rounds table mean?

# is the round number. The dice next to a name shows the dealer of the last round. In SkyJo, the padlock marks the player who closed the round, and “×2” a doubled score: a player who closes without having strictly the lowest score has their score doubled. Under the total, the trophy gives the number of rounds won and Ø the average per round.

How do I fix an entry mistake?

In the game, the “···” menu offers “Undo last round”: its scores are deleted and, if the game was over, it is reopened. You can also delete the whole game from this menu.

How do I end a Free game that the players decide to stop?

Tap “End game” in the bottom bar, then confirm: the current ranking becomes the final result. If you did it too early, the “···” menu offers “Reopen game”.

Game nights, statistics, report and data

What are game nights for?

A game night groups several games (for example a games evening or a tournament). Choose or create it when you start a game, then filter the game list and the statistics on it. The name of the game night is shown on each game that belongs to it.

How do I share the results of a game or a game night?

Tap “Share results” in the game, in the game list (after selecting the games you want) or in a game night: the app prepares a PDF, then opens the iOS share sheet (Messages, WhatsApp, Mail…). For free, the PDF contains the ranking and the results, with a link to the app's website. With Premium, the report is complete: statistics, round-by-round details and, from two finished games, a short debrief written with the intelligence built into the iPhone when available, otherwise with ready-made sentences. Nothing is sent before you choose the recipient.

Where is my data? How do I get it on another device?

Your games are saved on your device and synced through your iCloud, in a private space that only your Apple Account can access: Score Cartes has no account and no server. To get them on another iPhone or an iPad, sign it in to the same iCloud account; your games appear after a few moments. Without an iCloud account, everything stays on the device: then think of Apple's backups, or the direct iPhone-to-iPhone transfer.

My games are not syncing: what should I check?

Check that you are signed in to iCloud in the iPhone's Settings (Settings → iCloud in the app shows whether an account is detected), that the Internet connection is on, and that both devices use the same iCloud account. Syncing can take a few moments.

Which languages is the app available in?

French, English and German. It follows the device language, or you choose it in Settings → Language.

How do I permanently delete my data?

Swipe a game to the left in the list to delete it; the deletion carries over to your synced devices. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the device, but not the copy kept in your iCloud (iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud). The publisher holds no copy.

Which iPhone do I need?

Score Cartes runs on iPhone, with iOS 17 or later. The debrief written by the built-in intelligence needs a compatible device; other devices get a debrief made of ready-made sentences.
